## Shopping Strategy

### For New Players
1. **Start with affordable relics**: Gants de Fer (50 🪙), Écharpe du Vent (55 🪙)
2. **Focus on constitution**: Cœur de Titan for tanking
3. **Get basic healing**: Bandages Sacrés for survival

### For Experienced Players
1. **Mix and match**: Combine stat boosts for synergy
2. **Invest in damage**: Rune de Rage, Épée Ancienne
3. **Maximize defense**: Armure Ancienne, Bouclier Ancien
4. **Critical hits**: Talisman Critique, Œil du Faucon

## Relic Economy

- **Most Affordable**: Gants de Fer (50 🪙), Écharpe du Vent (55 🪙)
- **Most Expensive**: Œil du Faucon (100 🪙), Bouclier Ancien (105 🪙)
- **Best Value**: Many relics offer excellent combinations

- All relics are one-time purchases
- Relics remain equipped for the entire run
- Multiple relics can be equipped simultaneously
- Some relics have synergistic combinations
- Prices are in gold coins (🪙)
