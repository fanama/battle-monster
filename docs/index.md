## Relic Shopping Guide

### For New Players
- Start with affordable stat boosts: Gants de Fer (50 🪙), Écharpe du Vent (55 🪙)
- Focus on constitution for tanking: Cœur de Titan (60 🪙)
- Get basic healing: Bandages Sacrés (70 🪙)

### For Experienced Players
- Mix and match for synergy: Combine different stat types
- Invest in damage: Rune de Rage (75 🪙), Épée Ancienne (95 🪙)
- Maximize defense: Armure Ancienne (100 🪙), Bouclier Ancien (105 🪙)
- Critical hits: Talisman Critique (85 🪙), Œil du Faucon (100 🪙)

## Relic Categories

### Stat Boosts (16 relics)
Permanent increases to strength, speed, constitution, charisma, or wisdom.

### Healing (7 relics)
Percent of max HP healed at combat start.

### Defensive (6 relics)
Increases to Armor Class (CA).

### Damage & Lifesteal (6 relics)
Percent increases to damage and lifesteal.

### Critical Hits (2 relics)
Expands critical hit range.

### Experience (7 relics)
Percent increase to XP gained.
